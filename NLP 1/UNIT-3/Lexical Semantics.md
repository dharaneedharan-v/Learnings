
## **What is Lexical Semantics?**

**Lexical Semantics** is a subfield of linguistics that studies the **meaning of words** and their relationships with each other.

> Think of it as:  
> 🔍 "What do individual words mean, and how do they relate to other words?"

---

## 🧠 In Simple Terms:

Lexical = related to words  
Semantics = related to meaning

So **Lexical Semantics** = **"Word meaning."**

---

## 🎯 Why is it Used in NLP?

In NLP, **understanding the meaning of words** is critical for tasks like:

- Sentiment analysis
    
- Question answering
    
- Machine translation
    
- Chatbots
    
- Semantic search
    

Machines don't "understand" language like humans. So **Lexical Semantics** helps **teach machines how words carry meaning** and how they're **connected**.

---

## 🔍 What Does Lexical Semantics Study?

### 1. **Word Meaning**

- Different senses of a word
    
    > e.g., "bank" (riverbank vs. financial bank)
    

### 2. **Synonymy**

- Words that mean the same
    
    > e.g., _big_ ≈ _large_
    

### 3. **Antonymy**

- Opposite meanings
    
    > e.g., _hot_ vs _cold_
    

### 4. **Hyponymy & Hypernymy**

- **Hyponym**: a more specific word
    
    > e.g., "rose" is a hyponym of "flower"
    
- **Hypernym**: a more general word
    
    > e.g., "vehicle" is a hypernym of "car"
    
### 🧠 Easy Analogy: Parent and Child

- A **hyponym** is like a **child** in a category
    
- A **hypernym** is like a **parent** category

### 5. **Polysemy**

- One word, multiple related meanings
    
    > e.g., "head" (of a person, a company, a table)
    

### 6. **Homonymy**

- Words that **look/sound the same** but have **unrelated meanings**
    
    > e.g., _bat_ (animal) and _bat_ (used in cricket)
    

---

## 🧪 Example in NLP:

### **Problem**: You want to build a chatbot

User says:

> "I want to book a table."

You need to know:

- Are they talking about **dining** (restaurant)?
    
- Or **furniture**?
    

Lexical semantics helps by:

- Looking at the **context**
    
- Understanding **polysemy**
    
- Mapping **words to correct senses** (using WordNet or embeddings)
    

---

## 💻 How Is It Applied in NLP?

|Task|Role of Lexical Semantics|
|---|---|
|**Word Sense Disambiguation**|Choosing the correct meaning of a word in context|
|**Semantic Similarity**|Finding how close two words or phrases are in meaning|
|**Machine Translation**|Translating the right sense of a word|
|**Search Engines**|Matching similar or related terms|
|**Named Entity Recognition**|Understanding if a word is a place, person, or thing|

---

## 🛠 Tools and Resources Used:

- **WordNet** – A lexical database for English
    
- **GloVe / Word2Vec** – Word embeddings capturing word relationships
    
- **BERT** – Contextual understanding of word meaning
    
- **Lesk Algorithm** – For word sense disambiguation
    

---


## ✅ Final Thought:

> Lexical Semantics is **how machines start to "understand" words** — not just as strings, but as things that **mean something**, depending on context.
